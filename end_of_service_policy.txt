
COMPANY HR POLICY MANUAL
END OF SERVICE POLICY
Policy ID: HR-POL-002
Version: 1.0

==================================================
ARTICLE 1: PURPOSE
==================================================

This policy defines the company's internal procedure
for estimating end-of-service benefits for eligible
employees when their employment ends.

This policy is a fictional company policy created
for the HR Copilot project.

==================================================
ARTICLE 2: ELIGIBILITY
==================================================

2.1 Employees who have completed at least one year
of continuous service are eligible for the standard
company end-of-service benefit under this policy.

2.2 Employees with less than one year of service
must have their eligibility reviewed by HR.

2.3 HR must verify the employee's employment status
and service dates before calculating any benefit.

==================================================
ARTICLE 3: CALCULATION METHOD
==================================================

3.1 For this project, the illustrative company
calculation uses 21 days of basic salary for
each completed or partial year of service.

3.2 Daily basic salary is calculated as:

Daily Basic Salary = Monthly Basic Salary / 30

3.3 Estimated End-of-Service Benefit is calculated as:

Estimated Benefit =
Daily Basic Salary * 21 * Years of Service

3.4 Years of service are calculated using the
number of calendar days between the hire date
and the employment end date, divided by 365.25.

3.5 Only basic salary is included in this
illustrative calculation. Allowances are excluded.

==================================================
ARTICLE 4: REQUIRED INFORMATION
==================================================

4.1 HR must verify the following information:

- Employee ID
- Employment start date
- Employment end date
- Last basic monthly salary
- Employment status

4.2 The employee's salary and service dates must
be verified before issuing a final calculation.

==================================================
ARTICLE 5: REVIEW AND APPROVAL
==================================================

5.1 HR must review all end-of-service calculations.

5.2 The Finance Department must verify the
financial amount before payment.

5.3 Any exceptional case must be referred to
the HR Manager.

==================================================
ARTICLE 6: IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER
==================================================

6.1 This policy describes a fictional company
calculation used for software demonstration.

6.2 It is not a statement of Jordanian labor law
or any other country's employment legislation.

6.3 Actual legal entitlements may differ depending
on applicable law, contract terms, and circumstances.

6.4 Employees must consult HR for an official
end-of-service calculation.

==================================================
END OF END OF SERVICE POLICY
==================================================
