
COMPANY HR POLICY MANUAL
ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY
Policy ID: HR-POL-001
Version: 1.0

==================================================
ARTICLE 1: PURPOSE
==================================================

This policy defines the rules and procedures for
annual paid leave for company employees.

Its purpose is to ensure fair leave allocation,
proper workforce planning, and consistent leave
management across all departments.

==================================================
ARTICLE 2: ANNUAL LEAVE ENTITLEMENT
==================================================

2.1 Full-time employees are entitled to 21 working
days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

2.2 Annual leave entitlement is calculated on a
pro-rata basis for employees who join during the year.

2.3 Annual leave is accrued at a rate of 1.75 days
per completed month of service.

2.4 Part-time and temporary employees follow the
entitlement stated in their employment agreements.

==================================================
ARTICLE 3: LEAVE REQUEST PROCEDURE
==================================================

3.1 Employees must submit annual leave requests
through the company's HR system.

3.2 Employees should submit requests at least
5 working days before the requested leave date.

3.3 All leave requests require approval from the
employee's direct manager.

3.4 HR must record the final approval decision.

3.5 Approval depends on business requirements,
team availability, and operational needs.

==================================================
ARTICLE 4: LEAVE BALANCE
==================================================

4.1 Employees can check their remaining annual
leave balance through the HR department.

4.2 Remaining leave balance is calculated as:

Remaining Leave =
Annual Leave Entitlement - Approved Used Leave

4.3 Pending and rejected leave requests are not
deducted from the used leave balance.

4.4 Employees cannot use more annual leave days
than their available balance without special
written HR approval.

==================================================
ARTICLE 5: CARRY FORWARD
==================================================

5.1 Employees may carry forward a maximum of
5 unused annual leave days to the following year.

5.2 Carried-forward leave must be used by
March 31 of the following calendar year.

5.3 Unused carried-forward leave expires after
the deadline unless HR grants an exception.

==================================================
ARTICLE 6: LEAVE CANCELLATION
==================================================

6.1 Employees who wish to cancel an approved
leave request must notify their manager and HR.

6.2 HR must update the employee's leave record
after cancellation approval.

6.3 Cancelled leave must not be counted as
used annual leave.

==================================================
ARTICLE 7: MANAGEMENT RESPONSIBILITIES
==================================================

7.1 Managers are responsible for reviewing
leave requests fairly.

7.2 HR is responsible for maintaining accurate
employee leave records.

7.3 Employees are responsible for submitting
accurate leave requests and following this policy.

==================================================
ARTICLE 8: POLICY AUTHORITY
==================================================

8.1 HR is responsible for interpreting and
administering this policy.

8.2 Exceptions require written HR approval.

8.3 This document represents a fictional internal
company policy created for the HR Copilot project.
It is not a statement of applicable labor law.

==================================================
END OF ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY
==================================================
